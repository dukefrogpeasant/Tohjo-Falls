Pokémon Channel has several "Channels" you can watch (like a real tv).
These are the following:

Pichu Bros. In Party Panic: Pokemon episodes that have Pichu, Pichu and the others getting moon shaped cards. They're cards for Meowth's party!

Pokemon News Flash: This channel interviews Pokemon with random topics! Sort of like the TVs in the games.

Shop N Squirtle: You can buy Pokémon merch for Pikachu here, but also bus passes, minigames, a whole lot of stuff!

Wobbuffet Quiz: You can answer quizzes here to earn money. You get more money the faster you answer. If you're really fast you get 200, if you're just quick you get 150, if you're slow 100 and REALLY slow 50. If you're wrong you get NOTHING.

Slowpoke Weather: Slowpoke decides the weather REALLY slowly. The weather can cause different Pokémon to appear.

Eggzamination: Guess what pokemon is in the egg. If you're right you earn money. the more hours left before it hatches the more money.
